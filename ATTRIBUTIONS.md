# Attributions

## Counter-Strike 2 assets

The mod's first-person arm, glove, AK-47, and knife meshes; idle poses; material and skin textures; sleeve textures; inventory images; and icon come from a local installation of [Counter-Strike 2](https://store.steampowered.com/app/730/CounterStrike_2/). Counter-Strike 2 is developed and published by Valve. The rights to individual game assets may also belong to other contributors or licensors.

These assets were exported from the installed game and converted for this mod. They remain third-party content and are **not** covered by this project's [GPL license](LICENSE), which applies to the mod's original source code. See the [Steam Subscriber Agreement](https://store.steampowered.com/agreement/) for terms governing Steam game content.

## Extraction tool

[Source 2 Viewer / ValveResourceFormat](https://github.com/ValveResourceFormat/ValveResourceFormat) was used to export the CS2 models, animations, textures, and inventory images. It is a separate build-time tool, not included in the mod jar. The project is [MIT licensed](https://github.com/ValveResourceFormat/ValveResourceFormat/blob/master/LICENSE).

## Platform

The mod runs in [Minecraft: Java Edition](https://www.minecraft.net/) with [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/). Neither game nor loader is included in the mod jar.
